About Me

I am a research assistant at the Australian National University, working with Dr. ChongChong He on post-processing simulations run with QUOKKA, a GPU-accelerated radiation hydrodynamics code with adaptive mesh refinement. I completed my Bachelor of Science in Physics at ANU in 2025.

I am interested in generative modelling, particularly diffusion models and flow matching. My recent independent work explores flow matching for physical trajectory modelling and control.

Research & Projects

Independent project2026

Flow Matching for Physical Trajectories and Control

Derived and implemented a flow-matching formulation of DiffPhyCon’s joint trajectory model. Compared trajectory reconstruction and control with diffusion-based baselines on 1D Burgers and 2D Jellyfish tasks.

The experiments also investigate how guidance and prior reweighting behave under flow matching, and where an FM-specific derivation is needed.

Python Pipeline for Post-processing QUOKKA Simulations

Developing a Python pipeline to turn 3D QUOKKA simulation snapshots into synthetic emission-line observations using DESPOTIC, Grackle and Cloudy.

The research asks how reliably CO, Hα, C⁺ and H I trace the mass-weighted velocity dispersion of simulated gas, and how this depends on the gas phase.

Supervisor: Dr. ChongChong He
