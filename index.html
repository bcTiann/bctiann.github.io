About Me

I am a research assistant at the Australian National University, working with Dr. ChongChong He on post-processing simulations run with QUOKKA, a GPU-accelerated radiation hydrodynamics code with adaptive mesh refinement. I completed my Bachelor of Science in Physics at ANU in 2025.

I am drawn to AI because it opens up new ways to study things I did not previously know how to model—from images and language to the physical world. My current interests include diffusion models, flow matching, and spatial intelligence.

Research & Projects

Independent project2026

Flow Matching for Physical Trajectories and Control

Implemented Conditional OT Flow Matching for joint state–control generation in DiffPhyCon (NeurIPS 2024), a diffusion-based framework for physical system control. Evaluated it on 1D Burgers and 2D Jellyfish tasks alongside diffusion-based baselines.

Also explored objective guidance and control-prior reweighting through score-to-velocity adaptations. These experiments remain exploratory.

Python Pipeline for Post-processing QUOKKA Simulations

Research Assistant · April 2026–present

Developing a Python pipeline to turn 3D QUOKKA simulation snapshots into synthetic emission-line observations using DESPOTIC, Grackle and Cloudy.

The research asks how reliably CO, Hα, C⁺ and H I trace the mass-weighted velocity dispersion of simulated gas, and how this depends on the gas phase.

Supervisor: Dr. ChongChong He

Deep Learning for Cosmic Microwave Background Inpainting

Applied Transformers and diffusion models to reconstruct cosmic microwave background maps masked by the galactic plane. Evaluated reconstruction using the angular power spectrum, Cℓ.
