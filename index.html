About Me

I am a visiting student at CLEAR Lab, Southern University of Science and Technology, working with Dr. Xiong Zeng in Prof. Wei Zhang’s group on reinforcement learning for dexterous hand control and post-training of embodied foundation models.

I am also a research assistant at the Australian National University, working with Dr. ChongChong He on post-processing QUOKKA simulations. I completed my Bachelor of Science in Physics at ANU in 2025.

I am drawn to AI because it opens up new ways to study things I did not previously know how to model—from images and language to the physical world. My current interests include diffusion models, flow matching, and spatial intelligence.

Python Pipeline for Post-processing QUOKKA Simulations

Research Assistant · April 2026–present

I am developing a reusable Python post-processing pipeline for QUOKKA, a next-generation GPU-native astrophysical hydrodynamics code. It covers 3D data loading, chunked computation, analysis and visualization, producing synthetic images, spectra and statistics for comparison with astronomical observations and testing of physical models.

The pipeline combines DESPOTIC, Cloudy and analytic emission models, with thermal line broadening and dust attenuation.

I use these synthetic observations to investigate how reliably CO, Hα, C⁺ and H I line widths recover the mass-weighted velocity dispersion of simulated gas, and how this depends on the gas phase.

QUOKKA combines adaptive mesh refinement with radiation, magnetic fields, gravity, Grackle-based cooling and stellar feedback. The team’s primordial-star formation comparison reports a GPU speedup of over 50× relative to FLASH on CPUs for the same setup.

Supervisor: Dr. ChongChong He
